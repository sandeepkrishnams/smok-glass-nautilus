# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Discuss technical ideas without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project. Use small, non-sensitive examples and respect document authors' privacy.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation when needed. Report platform abuse through GitHub's reporting tools; avoid repeating private or harmful material in public threads.
